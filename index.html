COUNTING UNIT: POSITIVE-QUANTITY ORDER–EVENT RECORDS

Recorded transaction timing

Lead time is the scheduled event start minus the recorded transaction timestamp, in days, with each positive-quantity order–event record given equal weight. A negative value indicates a transaction recorded after the scheduled start and does not establish when someone attended or watched. The recorded DIGITAL_CATCHUP format includes different ticket labels, including ‘solo live’, so it should not automatically be interpreted as a uniform on-demand audience.

HOW TO READ THE RECORDS

Data notes

Different units, different populations

Ticket quantities come from the ticket-type extract. Timing uses all positive-quantity transaction records, including those absent from the ticket extract. Locations use distinct identified purchasers linked to ticket records. None of these measures establishes attendance or viewing.

Repeated rows and adjustments

Negative quantities may represent adjustments; their reasons are unconfirmed. After-start timestamps and zero-value records are retained.

Linkage and missing countries

Purchasers with an ID but no country remain ‘Unknown’. Rows without a purchaser ID are excluded from purchaser denominators. Countries are not inferred from postcodes.

Recorded classifications

‘UK’ is standardised to ‘United Kingdom’; ‘Usa’ and ‘United States of America’ to ‘United States’. ‘Other recorded location’ includes every other nonblank country label, including territories.
